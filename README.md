# Resident Access 0

A screen reader mod that makes **Resident Evil 0 HD Remaster** (Steam, PC) playable blind with an Xbox controller. It speaks through your screen reader (NVDA, JAWS, ZoomText, System Access and others), or Windows' own voice.

It is built from [Resident Access HD](https://github.com/RetroSanity/Resident-Access-HD), the mod for Resident Evil 1 HD Remaster. The two are separate downloads: this one needs Resident Evil 0 (re0hd.exe). If something tells you bhd.exe is missing, you have Resident Access HD instead of this mod.

## Download

Get the latest version from the [Releases page](../../releases/latest). Unzip it anywhere, run **Install Resident Access 0.bat**, then start the game from Steam. The mod starts with it and says "Resident Access 0 ready". It updates itself when a new version comes out.

## What it does (version 0.41)

- **Reads the game:** the title screen and menus, Load Game, every message in play (what you find, locked doors, questions with their Yes and No), the pause menu, the typewriter and every save slot as you move to it, and cutscene subtitles.
- **Puzzle screens:** the train brakes' number pads, the clock (its time as the hands move), which side of an examined item faces you as you turn it, and descriptions of pictures that carry a clue (the Microfilm Image's numbered seats).
- **Item screen:** each item with how many, "equipped", "Empty". With your partner, it says whose items you're looking at, and changing items with them reads their slots and how many you're passing over. Files are named and read in full.
- **Room names** as you go in, said once.
- **The list (the e list):** Doors (with stairs and ladders), Items (key items too), Things to check, Puzzles (things that ask you something, places to use an item, panels like the train brakes, lifts, and things you push, with how many pushes are left), Enemies with their health, and your partner. Everything is given with how far it is and which way, even with Rebecca and Billy in different rooms. Hold the left bumper and use the D-pad to go through it.
- **Auto walk:** takes you to anything in the list, then stops facing it so you can just press A. It uses stairs, and jumps down, climbs up and steps up at the game's own spots by itself. It takes you to the side to push a table or bookcase from, and climbs onto the table for you when that is the way. Hold B while it walks you to run. A beep plays while it walks you. Sometimes you may need to turn a little left or right, or step forward a little, before A works.
- **3D beacon:** a sound all round you leads you there if you'd rather walk yourself (use headphones).
- **Combat:** an aim beep when a shot would hit, and each enemy's health as you shoot it.
- **Levers:** "Holding" when you keep hold of one, and "Let go" when you move away.
- **Mod settings** in the Start menu.

The README.txt in the download explains every control.

## What you will need

- Resident Evil 0 / biohazard 0 HD REMASTER on Steam, on Windows 10 or 11
- A screen reader (or Windows' own voice)
- An Xbox controller

Set Control Type to **Original** in Game Setup, so auto walk and the 3D beacon work.
